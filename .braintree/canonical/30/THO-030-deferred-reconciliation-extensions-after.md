# Context

Gated on [[TAS-194-worktree-rebase-reconciliation-and-acceptance]].

The research also sketches cross-host transport and compare-and-swap, richer semantic redundancy and boundary-change assistance, signatures or authenticated policy, proposal and receipt retention, and garbage collection. None is required for the first two deployment scenarios.

# Outcome

Use measured evidence from the local and worktree phases to decide which deferred capabilities justify contract and implementation nodes, in what order, and which remain external policy or are rejected.

# Done when

- Measurements name proposal volume, overlap false positives, manual disposition effort, recovery behavior, and any coordination bottleneck left by the first two phases.
- Cross-host durability and serialization are separated from authentication and authorization, which Braintree must not silently claim.
- Semantic suggestions remain advisory and cannot automatically consolidate outcomes or alter node boundaries.
- Retention and garbage collection preserve the only records needed to explain accepted mappings and terminal dispositions.
- Each justified extension is admitted as one independently valuable implementation or definition node; unsupported ideas are explicitly disposed with rationale instead of expanding the core skill.
