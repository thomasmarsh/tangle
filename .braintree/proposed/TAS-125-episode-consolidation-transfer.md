# Context

Depends on [[TAS-123-pipeline-diagnostics]] at context_rev 1.

# Outcome

A minimal public episode shape and event-triggered consolidation policy earn adoption only if they help later agents reuse validated experience without unsupported generalization or negative transfer.

# Done when

- The candidate shape distinguishes situation, action, observable outcome, evidence, scope or confidence, and reusable lesson without storing private reasoning or transcripts.
- Consolidation triggers cover repeated failures, repeated successful workflows, contradictions, and costly resumptions.
- Every consolidated rule retains source evidence and procedural changes have executable or behavioral tests.
- Held-out transfer measures recurrence, steps, success, and negative transfer against the current node format.
