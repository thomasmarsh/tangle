# Context

Planning material is an intake input only when a consuming project identifies it as such, while Braintree is governed, versioned execution memory. This work must support project-declared sources and uses without presuming paths, roles, handling constraints, formats, audiences, integrations, or document structure, and without turning source-native units or inferred entities into automatic graph authority.

# Outcome

Projects can adopt Braintree around their own planning materials through a tested discovery and authority boundary, a resumable bridge where appropriate, incremental reviewed admission, and optional source-analysis, drift, and rendering capabilities whose applicability and authority remain explicit.

# Done when

- A project-neutral variability and discovery model states what is universal, project-declared, discoverable, unknown, or unsupported without requiring a representative corpus.
- A settled decision gives every mutable fact one owner in snapshot, living-specification, and full-migration modes.
- A bridge contract defines source identity, reviewed coverage, reconciliation, completion, and rollback.
- Manual pilots demonstrate incremental intake and measure resumption and dual-maintenance costs.
- The validated lifecycle is documented and contract-tested.
- The analyzer, drift, and renderer branches are implemented when justified or explicitly disposed with rationale.
- Every direct child is resolved or disposed, and this task records evidence that the lifecycle works end to end.
