Remaining tradeoffs

No database, daemon, custom indexer, or persistent helper was introduced. Numerical IDs can race; broad reverse-link, area, priority, and recency questions remain scans; an advisory Focus can go stale about intent; and timestamps rely on mutation discipline. These are explicit file-only costs, not hidden cache inconsistencies.
